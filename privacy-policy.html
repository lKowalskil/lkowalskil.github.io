/// THE SHORT VERSION

What every Nyxelium app has in common.

The individual policies are authoritative. This is the summary they all share.

NO ACCOUNT

Nothing to sign up for

Every app opens straight into use. No sign-up, no login, no email address handed over before the app works.

LOCAL BY DEFAULT

Your content stays on the device

Time entries, plants, pets, save files and watermark presets are written to local storage on the phone, not to a Nyxelium server.

DISCLOSED, NOT HIDDEN

Ads and analytics are named

Where a free version shows ads or uses consent-based analytics, the app's own policy says so and names the provider.

DELETION

Uninstalling removes it

Because app content lives on the device, uninstalling removes it. For anything held by a third-party service, email kowalski.dev07@gmail.com.
