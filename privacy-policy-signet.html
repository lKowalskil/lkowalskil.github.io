Privacy Policy

Effective date: 12 August 2026  ·  Last updated: 12 August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Signet (the “App”), published by Nyxelium (“we”, “us”, “our”), handles information when you use the App. Signet is an on-device batch photo watermarking app for Android (package com.nyxelium.signet).

The short version
  • Your selected photos, watermark text, logos, drafts and exports are processed on your device. We do not run a server that receives them, and we cannot see them.
  • Signet does not sell or share your personal data for advertising.
  • The initial Android release has no account, advertising, subscription or in-app purchase.
  • Analytics, crash reporting and performance collection are disabled in this release.
  • Original photos are not modified. Successful exports are saved as new files to a destination you choose.

1. Information processed on your device

Signet needs access to content you deliberately select in order to provide its watermarking features. This content remains on your device:

  • Selected photos — images you choose through Android’s system photo or file picker.
  • Watermark content — text, formatting, placement and transparent logo files you add.
  • Photo metadata — technical information such as dimensions, orientation, colour information and supported metadata required to read or create an export.
  • Draft and export state — local information needed to recover an interrupted job, report item results and retry failures.
  • App preferences — export and display settings stored locally.

Signet does not upload source photos, previews, watermark text, logo files, drafts or exported images to Nyxelium.

2. How selected media is accessed

Signet uses Android system pickers so you decide which photos or files the App may read. It does not request broad access to enumerate your entire photo library and does not request “all files” access.

Selected media is treated as read-only. Signet may make an app-owned working copy while a draft or export is active. Temporary files are used only to complete that job and are not repurposed for advertising, analytics, profiling or machine-learning training.

3. Exports and sharing

Every successful export is a new file. Signet does not overwrite or alter your original photo. In this release, exported files are saved to Android’s shared media collection so they can appear in compatible gallery and photo apps.

If you choose to share an export, Android’s system share interface lets you choose the receiving app or person. That transfer is initiated and controlled by you. The receiving service handles the file under its own privacy policy.

4. Data collected or transmitted by Nyxelium

The initial Android release does not transmit personal data or app-usage data to Nyxelium. In particular, it has:

  • no Signet account or login;
  • no advertising or advertising identifier access;
  • no subscription or in-app purchase;
  • no enabled analytics, crash-reporting or performance collection;
  • no cloud media processing or backup operated by Nyxelium.

Google Play and the Android operating system may process information independently to distribute, secure and operate apps. Their handling is governed by Google’s policies, not by a Signet application server.

6. Metadata

Photo metadata can contain sensitive information, including location or device details. Signet removes metadata by default where supported. If an export option explicitly lets you preserve supported metadata, the resulting file may include those values. Signet does not transmit those metadata values to Nyxelium.

7. Retention and deletion

Drafts, app-owned working copies and preferences remain locally only for as long as needed by the App’s workflow or until you remove them, clear Signet’s storage or uninstall the App. Exported files remain in the destination you selected until you delete them using Android or another file-management app.

Because Nyxelium does not receive this content, we cannot retrieve, restore or remotely delete it for you.

8. Security

Signet relies on Android’s application sandbox, system content interfaces and scoped media storage to limit access. Device backups and device-to-device transfer of Signet’s private working data are disabled in this release. No method of local storage is completely risk-free, so keep your device and operating system protected and up to date.

9. Children’s privacy

Signet is intended for photographers, creators, online sellers and other general productivity users. It is not directed to children under 13, or the equivalent minimum age in the user’s country, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

10. Your rights and requests

Depending on where you live, privacy law may provide rights over personal data. Most Signet content stays only on your device, where you control it directly. If you have a question or believe information has reached us through a support request, contact us using the address below.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when Signet’s features, service providers or legal requirements change. We will update the date at the top of this page and provide additional notice where required. We will revise this policy and the applicable store disclosures before enabling advertising, purchases or remote diagnostics in a future release.
