/// EDITORIAL CONTRACT

Written from the product, not around keywords.

Each guide starts with a real user question. Search phrases help us choose the wording, but they do not decide the answer. We check every product claim against the current app and link to Google Play only when the app is relevant to the problem.

The workshop notes follow the same contract with no product at the end of them. They cover the tools around the work — graphics cards, Windows, backups, developer setup, devices — and they name the better option whenever there is one, including when it is not the thing the guide is about.

External claims are dated and sourced, because hardware and tooling move faster than any page written about them. These guides will keep expanding as the apps evolve and as readers ask better questions.
